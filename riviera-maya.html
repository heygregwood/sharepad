🏖️ Option 1: The Secure Marina (Puerto Aventuras)

15 min travel Family-Safe

Since the resort is at capacity, this gated community is the safest "all-in-one" alternative. You do not need a pass to enter the marina area.

The Experience:

Stroll the luxury marina, browse upscale boutiques, and watch the resident dolphins.

Dining:

Visit The Nauti Burro for waterfront tacos or Pelican Point for a high-end seafood lunch.

🛡️ Safety: 24/7 guarded gate access makes this the most controlled environment for families.

🤿 Option 2: The Nature Inlet (YAL-KU Lagoon)

12 min travel Nature & Snorkel

We stayed near here last year.

The Experience:

Crystal-clear inlet where the sea meets fresh cenote water.

Best Move:

Rent a Private Palapa early (9:00 AM) to give the family a shaded home base for the morning.

💰 Cost: Entrance is ~$300 MXN (~$18 USD) per person plus gear rentals.

🌆 Option 3: The Urban Hub (Playa del Carmen)

35 min travel Shopping & Nightlife

The heartbeat of the Riviera Maya. Head here if the family wants shopping and a big-city beach energy.

Day Scene:

Mamita's Beach Club for a high-end pool/beach experience.

Evening Scene:

Coco Bongo for world-class acrobatics and nightlife.

🚐 Logistics: Tell the shuttle to drop you at "Quinta Avenida and Calle 12."
