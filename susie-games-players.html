🪨 Rock Stack Challenge

📋 How to Play:

  1. Collection Phase: When Susie starts the timer, you have limited time to collect as many rocks as you can find
  2. Stacking Phase: You get 3 tries to stack your rocks as high as possible
  3. Scoring: Your highest stack counts - Susie counts the rocks
  4. Bonus Points: If your stack stays up for 30 seconds, you get +5 points!

💡 Pro Tips:

  • Look for flat rocks - they stack better!
  • Start with bigger rocks on the bottom
  • Take your time - rushing makes stacks fall

🌈 Color Hunt

📋 How to Play:

  • Goal: Find natural objects in these specific colors:
  • 🔴 RED
  • ⚫ BLACK
  • ⚪ WHITE
  • 🔵 BLUE
  • 🟢 GREEN
  • 🟡 YELLOW
  • Rule: Objects must fit in the palm of your hand
  • Scoring: 5 points per color found
  • BONUS: Find ALL 6 colors for +10 bonus points (40 total!)

💡 Pro Tips:

  • Flowers are great for colors!
  • Don't forget leaves can be different colors
  • Rocks and dirt count for black/white/brown
  • Look for berries for red/blue
⏱️ No timer for this game - take your time and find all the colors!

🐌 Slime Time

📋 How to Play:

  • Time Limit: 8 minutes to find slimy things
  • Goal: Find the SLIMIEST natural things you can!
  • What to Look For:
    • Slugs 🐌
    • Snails 🐚
    • Wet moss or algae
    • Slimy rocks (near water)
    • Mud
    • Slime mold
  • THE RULE: You MUST touch each item to prove it's slimy! 🤢
  • Scoring: 3 points per slimy item
  • Bonus: Susie judges the SLIMIEST - winner gets +10 points!

💡 Pro Tips:

  • Check near water for extra slime!
  • Under rocks and logs are great hiding spots
  • Wet = more likely to be slimy
  • Don't be scared - it's just nature! 😄

🔍 Evidence Hunter

📋 How to Play:

  • Time Limit: 10 minutes to find evidence
  • Goal: Find evidence of animals, plants, or weather in nature
  • What to Look For:
    • 🐾 Animal tracks or footprints
    • 🍃 Chewed or damaged leaves
    • 💩 Animal scat (poop)
    • 🌰 Eaten nuts or seeds
    • 🪺 Nests or feathers
    • 🪨 Erosion or weathering on rocks
    • 🍂 Decomposing plants
    • 🌱 New plant growth
  • Scoring: 3 points per piece of evidence
  • BONUS: +5 points if you can explain WHAT caused it and HOW!

💡 Pro Tips:

  • Look carefully - evidence can be small!
  • Think like a scientist - what made this mark?
  • Under logs and rocks often have lots of evidence
  • Be ready to explain your evidence to Susie!
