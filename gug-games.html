THE GUG GAMES

Tulum Edition

10 Minutes. 3 Tasks. One Winner.

MISSION TIMER
10:00

THE RULES

  • Do not run in hallways or slippery areas
  • You must complete all three tasks before returning to the Judge
  • The first person to show the Judge all completed items/photos wins
1

Visual ID

Find and take a photo of the following three things:

  • Something that is older than you
  • A sign with the letter 'Q' or 'Z' on it
  • A reflection of yourself in something that isn't a mirror
2

The Intel

Gather information by asking questions:

  • Ask a member of the staff (or a nearby adult) what their favorite Mexican snack is
  • Find out the name of the nearest Cenote
💡 Be polite and introduce yourself as "Agent on a scavenger hunt"
3

Retrieval

Find and bring back these physical items:

  • A leaf that looks like an animal (you must explain which one)
  • A perfectly round stone
🌿 Remember: Leave no trace. Only collect items from the ground.

Once all 3 tasks are complete:

🏆

MISSION COMPLETE!

Show the Judge all your evidence:

✓ Photos (Visual ID)
✓ Intel answers
✓ Physical items
